        2. carry appropriate water, sun protection, clothing and footwear, and a charged phone;

        3. keep a safe, respectful distance from wildlife, and never approach, corner, chase, feed, handle or bait a bird (or any other animal) to get a better look or a photo;

        4. stay clear of nesting and breeding sites, because disturbing them can cause birds to abandon eggs or chicks;

    2. The Air 50

      1. Spot and log the 50 Official Species within the Boundary. There's no entry deadline and no time limit — it's an ongoing, go-at-your-own-pace challenge you can pick up any week of the year, starting on the Launch Date and subject to clause 16.

      2. Anyone may take part — backyard birders, families out for a walk, seasoned twitchers with a scope, provided they register an Account under clause 4. There's no entry fee, no purchase is necessary and no equipment requirement, though a pair of binoculars and a field guide certainly help.

      3. The Boundary is the Brisbane City Council area plus the Moreton Bay, Redland City, Logan City and Ipswich City council areas, including Moreton Island and North Stradbroke Island. A Sighting anywhere inside the Boundary counts.

      4. Any map of the Boundary on the Website is an illustrated guide, not a survey-accurate boundary. If you're near the edge and unsure, log it anyway, and we will decide, acting reasonably, whether the Sighting was made within the Boundary.

      5. To count towards your tally, a Sighting must:

        1. be of a wild, free-living bird, and not a captive, aviary, zoo, or clearly domestic bird (backyard chickens, farmed ducks);

        2. be of a bird you observed in person, within the Boundary, on or after the Launch Date;

        3. include a Sighting Photo that complies with clause 12.1;

        4. be submitted using your Display Name and PIN; and

        5. be genuine and accurate.

      6. Each species counts once per Participant. Once you've logged a Rainbow Lorikeet, a second Rainbow Lorikeet doesn't add to your tally — though you're welcome to keep a personal log of repeat Sightings.

      7. Use the common name from the Species Checklist where you can. A species not on that list still counts towards your overall tally, as long as you're confident of the identification, but only Official Species count towards Air Points, the Hall of Fame and the Finisher's Coin.

      8. Submit each Sighting through the Log a Sighting page with your Display Name and PIN, the species, the date, roughly where you saw it, and a clear photo as evidence that complies with clause 12.1. A Sighting Photo is required, not optional.

      9. Every submission starts as pending — it won't appear on the Leaderboard or count toward your tally until it's been reviewed and confirmed, either by an automated check (most Sightings, within moments) or by our manual review. Manual reviews usually take no more than 48 hours, so a pending Sighting isn't a sign anything's wrong — it just hasn't been looked at yet. You can check the status of your own Sightings any time on the My Sightings page.

      10. The Brisbane Air 50 only counts Sightings from the Launch Date onward. Every Sighting Photo is checked against its original date and time stamp (EXIF metadata) — if that timestamp is from before the Launch Date, the Sighting won't be confirmed, even if the photo is completely genuine.

      11. This exists so nobody can complete the challenge on day one from an old photo collection — the challenge is about what you spot from launch onward, not what you've already got saved. Submissions that fail the authenticity or timestamp check are rejected with a reason and can be resubmitted with a valid photo. We will decide, acting reasonably, whether a Sighting is confirmed, and our decision is final.

      12. Each Sighting Photo is also checked automatically when it is submitted, including by the image analysis service described in the Privacy Policy, which checks that it appears to be a genuine, unedited photograph of a bird. A Sighting is rejected automatically, and we will tell you why, if the location recorded in its Sighting Photo is outside the Boundary, if the Sighting Photo has no readable capture date and time, or if the capture date is before the Launch Date.

      13. You complete the Air 50 when we have confirmed a Sighting of each of the 50 Official Species and carried out a final review of those Sightings. Your Completion Time is the date and time, as recorded by our systems, at which we received the Sighting that completed your 50 Official Species, provided that Sighting and each of your other Official Species Sightings is confirmed. Once we receive that Sighting, your Display Name, Avatar, any Founding Flock badge, species tally and Air Points, and Completion Time will appear on the Hall of Fame marked as pending final review until our final review is complete.
