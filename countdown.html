How it works

01. Go spot birds

Anywhere in Greater Brisbane

Your backyard, New Farm Park, Moreton Island, the D'Aguilar Range — anywhere within the Greater Brisbane boundary counts. Keep an eye and an ear out.

02. Log what you see

One entry per species

Record the species, date and suburb on the Log a Sighting page once the site's live. Only your first sighting of each species adds to your tally.

03. Chase the fifty

Climb the leaderboard

Track your tally on the Leaderboard. Log all 50 official Brisbane Air 50 species and your name goes up in the Hall of Fame.
