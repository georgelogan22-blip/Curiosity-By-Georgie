    Privacy Policy

    How we handle your personal information when you register, take part in a Challenge, or otherwise use the Website. The plain-language summary is on the Register page — this is the complete document behind it.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 16 November 2026

    1. About this policy

      1. This Privacy Policy explains how George Logan trading as Curiosity by Georgie (we, us or our) collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information in connection with curiositybygeorgie.au and the challenges we run on it. We handle personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

      2. Capitalised terms used in this Privacy Policy that are not defined in it have the meaning given in our Website Terms and Challenge Rules (Terms).

    2. Why we collect personal information

      1. We ask you to register because of the real risks involved in each Challenge (see the Safety first clause of the Terms) and so that we're able to permanently ban anyone who causes abuse or harm to other Participants or to wildlife and nature. Your name and postal address, which we only ask for if you become entitled to a Reward, are also how we post out a Finisher's Coin, if you're one of the first fifty people to complete all fifty Official Species.

      2. More generally, we collect, use and disclose personal information to:

        1. register and manage your Account, including confirming your age and, if you are a Minor, obtaining your Parent's consent;

        2. run and verify each Challenge, including reviewing Sightings, checking that Sightings were made within the Boundary and that Sighting Photos are genuine, and maintaining the Leaderboard and Hall of Fame;

        3. deliver Rewards;

        4. feature Sighting Photos on the Announcements Feed or our social media accounts, with consent;

        5. communicate with you about your participation, including changes to a Challenge or the Terms and safety notices;

        6. enforce the Terms and the Community Guidelines, including identifying any person who has been banned;

        7. respond to enquiries, requests and complaints;

        8. operate, secure and improve the Website; and

        9. comply with our legal obligations.

      3. If you do not give us the personal information we request at registration, we will not be able to register you.

      4. Some decisions about Sightings are made automatically. When you submit a Sighting, computer programs use your Sighting Photo and the information embedded in it, including its location and capture date and time, to check that it was taken within the Boundary on or after the Launch Date and that it appears to be a genuine, unedited photograph of a bird rather than a drawing, screenshot or stock image. A Sighting that fails these checks is rejected automatically, and we tell you why. Other decisions, including the final review before a Participant enters the Hall of Fame, are made by us.

      5. We will only send you news or updates about future challenges if you have agreed to receive them, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We will not send marketing to Minors or use a Minor's personal information for marketing or targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information.

    3. Information that is publicly visible

      1. Only your Display Name and Avatar are ever shown publicly as your identity, on the Leaderboard and Hall of Fame. Your full name, date of birth, postal address, email address and PIN are never published.

      2. The following information is publicly visible on the Website, and through its public application programming interface, to anyone who visits: your Display Name and Avatar, your species tally and Air Points, your most recent Sighting date, any badges, your Hall of Fame induction date (including, while your final review is pending, a notice to that effect and your Completion Time), and any Sighting Photo that is featured with consent under clause 3.5. Everything else is not published and is not available through the public application programming interface.

      3. Your Sighting Photos and the location of each Sighting are visible only to us and to you, through your own PIN-protected dashboard, unless a Sighting Photo is featured with your consent.

      4. We remove location information embedded in Sighting Photos and uploaded Avatars when they are uploaded, before they are displayed or sent to any of our service providers.

      5. If you, or your Parent if you are a Minor, agree to a Sighting Photo being featured on the Announcements Feed or our social media accounts, we will publish that Sighting Photo with your Display Name only. You can withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us, and we will remove the Sighting Photo within 7 days.

      6. Anything that is publicly visible may be copied or shared by others, and we cannot control that. Please choose your Display Name carefully, and do not include personal details in it.

    4. What personal information we collect

      1. When you register an Account, we collect your Display Name, full name, date of birth, email address and Avatar and the PIN you choose, and, if you choose to give it, a contact phone number. If you are a Minor, we also collect your Parent's full name and email address, and a record of your Parent's consent.

      2. When you take part in a Challenge, we collect your Sightings (including the species, date, location and any notes), your Sighting Photos and the information embedded in them (such as the date, time and location at which the photo was taken, and the device used), and your progress and results.

      3. When you upload a Sighting Photo, we record the GPS location embedded in it privately before removing it from the Sighting Photo. We use that location only to check that the Sighting was made within the Boundary. It is not shown to anyone, including you, and we delete it once the Sighting has been reviewed.

      4. If you, or your Parent if you are a Minor, agree to a Sighting Photo being featured, we collect the full legal name, email address and date of birth of the person giving consent, as a record of that consent.

      5. If you become entitled to a Reward, we collect the postal address to which the Reward is to be sent. If you contact us, we collect your name, your email address and the content of your message.

      6. When you use the Website, our website host, GitHub, automatically records technical information, such as your IP address, device and browser type, and the pages you visit. We do not use cookies or analytics tools.

      7. We collect personal information directly from you, from your Parent if you are a Minor, and automatically when you use the Website. We do not intend to collect sensitive information, such as information about your health, and we ask you not to include sensitive information in your User Content.

      8. You can browse the Website without telling us who you are, and your Display Name allows you to take part under a pseudonym. However, we need your real name, date of birth and email address to register you, because we need to confirm your age, obtain parental consent where required, and be able to identify any person we have banned under the Terms.

    5. Who we disclose personal information to

      1. We store personal information with Supabase, our database provider, on servers located in Australia.

      2. We also disclose personal information to GitHub, which hosts the Website, Resend, which sends emails on our behalf, Anthropic, which provides the image analysis service we use to check Sighting Photos, and Australia Post and other delivery services (including overseas postal services), which deliver Rewards.

      3. We may also disclose personal information:

        1. to a Minor's Parent, in relation to the Minor's Account;

        2. to police, emergency services, regulators and other authorities where required or authorised by law, or where we reasonably believe it is necessary to lessen or prevent a serious threat to any person's life, health or safety;

        3. to our professional advisers; and

        4. to any person who takes over the operation of the Website or the Challenges.

      4. Our database is hosted in Australia. GitHub, Resend and Anthropic store or process personal information in the United States. We take reasonable steps to ensure that any overseas recipient handles personal information in a way that is consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

    6. Children and young people

      1. Minors may take part in a Challenge with their Parent's consent. We collect only the personal information we need to run the Challenge safely, and we consider the best interests of children in how we handle their information. When a Minor registers, we email their Parent to ask them to confirm their consent, and the Minor's Account remains pending until their Parent confirms.

      2. A Parent may ask to access, correct or delete their child's personal information, and may withdraw their consent at any time by contacting us. If a Parent withdraws their consent, we will close the Minor's Account.

      3. We never publish a Minor's real name, and we encourage Minors to choose a Display Name that does not identify them. We will only feature a Sighting Photo submitted by a Minor if their Parent has given consent. Minors cannot upload their own image as an Avatar and must choose from the images we provide.

      4. We will review this Privacy Policy when the Children's Online Privacy Code takes effect.

    7. Security and retention

      1. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure, including by limiting access to people who need it to run the Challenges and using reputable service providers. No method of storing or transmitting information is completely secure, so please keep your PIN confidential.

      2. If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm to any individual, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner where required by law.

      3. We keep personal information for as long as your Account is active and for 2 years after your Account is closed or the relevant Challenge ends, unless we need to keep it for longer to comply with the law or to enforce a ban under the Terms. We delete postal addresses 12 months after the relevant Reward is delivered. We keep the record of a consent to feature a Sighting Photo for as long as the Sighting Photo is featured and for 2 years afterwards. When we no longer need personal information, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it.

    8. Access, correction and complaints

      1. You may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us through the contact page on the Website or by email at curiositybygeorgie@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. We may refuse access where permitted by law, in which case we will tell you why. We do not charge for access requests.

      2. If you have a concern or complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us. We will acknowledge your complaint promptly and aim to respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au or by calling 1300 363 992.

      3. We may update this Privacy Policy by publishing the updated version on the Website. If we make a material change, we will notify Participants by email or by a notice on the Website.

    Questions about your personal information? Get in touch and Georgie will help.
