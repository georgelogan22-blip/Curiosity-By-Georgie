    Register to Play

    One-time sign up, before your first sighting

    The Brisbane Air 50 happens outdoors, around real wildlife, so we ask everyone to register properly before they start logging sightings — see why, and what happens to your information. It only takes a minute.

    Limited — first 100 only
    The Founding Flock

    The first 100 people to register become the Founding Flock — a badge that appears next to your name on the Leaderboard and Hall of Fame the moment you sign up, and stays there for good, whether or not you ever finish a challenge. Go on to complete the full Brisbane Air 50 as one of the Founding Flock and there's a second thank-you waiting for you too — we're not saying what it is yet. It stacks with the finisher's coin (see Finisher Rewards) if you're also one of the first fifty to finish, so it's entirely possible to earn both.

    Registration only opens once the challenge itself launches — there's no way to register early to improve your odds. From that moment, it's first in, first served.

    Before You Start
    Why we ask for this

    Registering is required because of the real risks involved in the challenge (see Rule #1 — Safety First) and so that we're able to permanently ban anyone who causes abuse or harm to other participants or to wildlife and nature. Your full name and address are also how we post out a finisher's coin, if you're one of the first fifty people to complete all fifty species — see Finisher Rewards for why only fifty will ever exist.

    Only your display name is ever shown publicly — on the Leaderboard and Hall of Fame. Your legal name, date of birth, address, phone, and email are never published; they're stored for registration records and coin delivery only. Full detail in Your Privacy, below.

    Please Read
    Your Privacy

    What we collect: your display name, full legal name, date of birth, address, phone, and email — once, here at registration — plus a parent/guardian consent tickbox if you're under 18.

    What's public: only your display name, species tally, and most recent sighting date, on the Leaderboard and Hall of Fame. Everything else is never published or available through the public API — only Georgie, as project admin, can see it. Your address is used to post a finisher's coin if you're among the first fifty to complete all fifty; the rest is kept as a registration record, mainly so anyone who harms a participant or wildlife can be identified and banned.

    Data is stored with Supabase, hosted in Australia. To correct, remove, or ask what's held about you, get in touch. This isn't formal legal advice — the registration flow should be reviewed against the Australian Privacy Act before launch.
