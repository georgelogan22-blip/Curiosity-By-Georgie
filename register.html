Register to Play

One-time sign up, before your first sighting

The Brisbane Air 50 happens outdoors, around real wildlife, so we ask everyone to register properly before they start logging sightings — see why, and what happens to your information. It only takes a minute.

Before You Start
Why we ask for this

Registering is required because of the real risks involved in the challenge (see Rule #1 — Safety First) and so that we're able to permanently ban anyone who causes abuse or harm to other participants or to wildlife and nature. Your full name and address are also how we post out a finisher's coin if you complete all fifty.

Only your display name is ever shown publicly — on the Leaderboard and Hall of Fame. Your legal name, date of birth, address, phone, and email are never published; they're stored for registration records and coin delivery only. Full detail in Your Privacy.
