Greater Brisbane · Citizen Birding Challenge

The Brisbane Air 50

Fifty wild bird species. No entry fee, no timer — just your eyes, a notebook, and the river city's flyways. Log every species you spot and see how close you get to the fifty.

How it works

01 — Go spot birds

Anywhere in Greater Brisbane

Your backyard, New Farm Park, Moreton Island, the D'Aguilar Range — anywhere within the Greater Brisbane boundary counts. Keep an eye and an ear out.

02 — Log what you see

One entry per species

Record the species, date and suburb on the Log a Sighting page. Only your first sighting of each species adds to your tally.

03 — Chase the fifty

Climb the leaderboard

Track your tally on the Leaderboard. Reach 50 unique species and your name goes up in the Hall of Fame.
