    The Founding Flock — first 100 registrations

    Separate from the coin: the first 100 people to register get a Founding Flock badge next to their name on the Leaderboard and Hall of Fame, for good, from the moment they sign up — see Register. Go on to complete the full Brisbane Air 50 as one of them and there's a second, undisclosed thank-you waiting. It stacks with the coin above: register early and finish inside the first fifty, and you keep both rewards in full — one doesn't come at the cost of the other.

    How you earn one

    Log and confirm all 50 official Brisbane Air 50 species and you're eligible for the Hall of Fame — no cap, no matter when you finish. The coin is different: only the first fifty people inducted receive one, in the order Georgie confirms them. If you're among them, once your induction goes up Georgie posts your coin to the address you gave at registration — engraved with your display name, and numbered by your place in that first fifty.

    Only fifty, ever

    No reprint, no second batch, no exceptions. Once fifty are gone, you won't get a #051. There's no way to buy, win, or request one outside the challenge itself: the only way in is confirming all 50 species inside the first fifty Hall of Famers. Reserved for people who've done something genuinely impressive, A priceless artifact that costs you nothing. Finish outside the first fifty and you're still rightfully in the Hall of Fame — just without a coin.

    The same rule will hold for every other Brisbane 250 discipline as they launch: fifty coins each, for that discipline's own first fifty finishers, and nothing more.

    Why the front is hidden

    The back is final, so there's nothing to spoil — it's shown as-is above. The front's design stays behind the silhouette site-wide until the first participant reaches Air Veteran (40 confirmed species), at which point it's revealed here for everyone, finisher or not. Reaching Air Veteran doesn't get you a coin on its own — that still takes finishing all 50 inside the first fifty — but it does mean the wait for what the coin looks like is over.
